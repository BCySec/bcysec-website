Another Bocconi course evaluation survey lands in your inbox. A recruiter sends a link to an internship application. A friend urgently asks for money through a Revolut link. Each could be legitimate… or an attempt to exploit your trust. 🤔

October is European Cybersecurity Month, a campaign raising awareness about digital security. We’re taking this opportunity to explore what cybersecurity means and why it matters in our everyday lives.

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting digital systems, networks, and information from unauthorized access, damage, and disruption. It helps keep your information confidential, accurate, and available when needed. Your personal details should stay private, your submitted work should remain unchanged, and your course materials should be accessible.

Cybersecurity involves backing up files, updating software, and making suspicious activity easy to report. It also involves managers deciding how much to invest in protection, finance professionals verifying transactions, lawyers addressing responsibilities around data, and policymakers considering the security of public services. Whatever your discipline, understanding cyber risk helps you make better decisions.

You can start with a few simple habits: use strong, unique passwords, enable multifactor authentication, keep your devices updated, and report suspicious activity. If a message pressures you to act urgently, pause and verify the request through a separate, trusted channel. These actions help protect both you and the people around you.

This October, take a moment to review how you protect your digital life. Understanding cybersecurity begins with recognizing what you value, how it could be put at risk, and what you can do to protect it.
