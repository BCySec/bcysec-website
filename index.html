Our mission

Built by students, for the students who'll defend tomorrow's systems.

Cybersecurity is now a business and legal problem as much as a technical one, and Bocconi has no dedicated space for students to engage with it outside a specific Master's program. BCySec is that space: open to every degree and every year, built through workshops, panels, field visits, and case studies with people who actually work in the field. When this year's AI Games included a cybersecurity component, Bocconi students turned out for it — the interest is already here, it just didn't have a home yet.

What we do

Six commitments, straight from our statute.

BCySec exists to:

Awareness & literacy

Promote cybersecurity awareness and digital literacy across every degree program at Bocconi.

Bridging the gap

Connect technical cybersecurity knowledge with its business, legal, and strategic implications.

Hands-on programming

Run workshops, field visits, seminars, and case studies on real cybersecurity topics.

Real connections

Connect students directly with cybersecurity professionals, firms, and institutions.

Ethics first

Build an ethics-first culture, in line with university, Italian, and EU regulations.

Collaboration

Work with other student associations, academic departments, and outside organizations.

What's ahead

A first year, in three parts.

Fall 2026

A kickoff event introducing cybersecurity to new members, followed by an awareness month with industry guests and a firm visit in Milan.

Winter 2026/27

A business case-study series on how managers and executives respond to real cyber incidents, closing with a guest speaker from industry or law.

Spring 2027

A networking evening with Bocconi alumni, outreach to peer associations at other European universities, a member-led workshop, and a showcase to close out our first year.
