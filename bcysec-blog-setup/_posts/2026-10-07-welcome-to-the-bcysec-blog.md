This is where BCySec will publish write-ups, event recaps, and anything the team wants to share with the wider Bocconi community — alongside the main site, which stays focused on who we are and how to join.

Feel free to edit or delete this post — it's just here as a working example so the format is clear before the first real article goes up.

## What belongs here

Anything the team wants to put in front of students: thoughts on a talk or workshop, a recap after an event, something one of us has been reading about in cybersecurity, or updates on what BCySec is working on.

## How to publish

See the included guide, `How-to-publish-a-blog-post.md`, for the exact steps — it only takes creating one text file in the repository, no coding involved.
